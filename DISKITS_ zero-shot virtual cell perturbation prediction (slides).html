DISKITS

Predict what a gene knockdown does in a cell type nobody has run the experiment in.

A zero-shot framework for the 2026 Virtual Cell Challenge: CRISPRi responses in six held-out cell contexts, predicted from their untouched control cells alone. The system is fully specified and the CPU compute is secured. We are asking for 100 H100 hours to turn it into results.

DISKITS carries what is known about knockdowns in other cell lines into contexts it has only seen as untouched controls. A new cell type becomes a computation, not a campaign.

Strong foundations exist. The opening is the unseen cell type.

scGen, CPA2,3Autoencoders that learn perturbation shifts in a latent space. Cell type is typically a fixed category, so a new one has no description.
GEARS4A graph network over gene-gene knowledge that predicts unseen gene targets, usually within the cell lines it was trained on.
Foundation models5,6scGPT, Geneformer and similar give rich pretrained cell representations. A 2025 benchmark found simple linear baselines hard to beat on perturbation tasks.7
STATE8Arc Institute's transformer, trained on over 100 million perturbed cells, with predictions in novel cellular contexts. The closest relative, and a clear sign the problem is ripe.

DISKITS builds on these ideas and adds what zero-shot contexts need: an explicit context descriptor, regulatory priors, and gates against simple baselines.

A label cannot describe a cell type nobody has seen. A landscape can.

The problem

Many models describe a cell type with a category or an embedding learned in training. A cell type absent from training has no entry to look up.

The attractor field

Fit 16 stable cell states once. Any context, seen or not, becomes its occupancy: the share of its own control cells in each state. Controls are all it needs.

Why it travels

Same states, new mix. Knockdown effects from K562 or RPE1 are re-weighted by how closely a new context's occupancy matches theirs.9

A new cell type stops being a missing category and becomes a point in a shared landscape.

Step 1: squeeze each cell into 128 numbers

A negative-binomial autoencoder15 reads 18,533 gene counts per cell and compresses them into a 128-number summary.

The decoder is one linear map, so a shift in the summary maps straight back to gene-level changes.

Step 2: cell states are wells. A knockdown reshapes the landscape.

Each stable state is a well in a potential landscape1,16,17 (5 drawn here, 16 in the model). The deeper the well, the more of the control cells sit in it. That share is the occupancy.

A knockdown deepens some wells, shallows others and nudges their centres. Cells roll to the new minima, and the shift decodes into gene changes.

Step 3: how a knockdown moves π(c)

Two phases: learn on sources, adapt on controls

Response labels only ever shape phase 1. A held-out context contributes control cells to phase 2 alone. Where the rules allow, control cells may also join the control-only representation fit.

Biology supplies a map for unfamiliar targets

Regulatory edgesSigned CollecTRI10 TF-to-target links combine with SCENIC11 networks inferred from eligible control cells.
Protein featuresESM212 sequence embeddings summarize each target protein; missing sequences receive zero features.
Pathway featuresReactome13 membership represents shared biological programs across genes.
Target similaritySTRING links, pathways, protein similarity, regulator sets, and source-response profiles help retrieve related targets.

The gene representation has 132 standardized features. Fold-specific network construction keeps held-out context cells out of graph-derived information.

Four evidence streams cover different levels of support

S1Learned response. The graph-conditioned head predicts state-occupancy and latent shifts for this target and context.
S2Same target. Transfer observed effects from source contexts with similar attractor occupancy.
S3Related targets. Retrieve effects from the 20 most similar genes and weight them by similarity.
S4Regulatory propagation. Spread a knockdown prior through signed regulatory edges for three decaying steps.

A non-negative stacker blends streams by target-support tier. If a tier fails to beat knockdown-only in inner validation, its learned stream weights are set to zero.

Support tiers decide which streams speak

Tier A

The target has an observed effect in at least one source screen, so same-target transfer (S2) is available.

Tier B

No direct data, but a similar gene clears the similarity threshold, chosen on inner folds only. Neighbor transfer (S3) steps in.

Tier C

Neither exists. Only the learned head and regulatory spread contribute, and they are shrunk to zero if they cannot beat knockdown-only.

Convert predicted log1p effects back to counts. The submission stays in raw-count form and is not rescaled to a median UMI target.

Selection follows held-out performance, not a proxy

Nested evaluation

Inner context folds select checkpoints and settings. Outer held-out contexts estimate generalization without tuning on their results.

Official objective

Rendered predictions use the verified official cell-eval aggregate. Training losses update neural weights but do not choose the checkpoint.

Simple baselines stay in play

Compare knockdown-only, same-target transfer, shared response, shuffled targets, and dictionary-size variants.

Gates can disable complexity

Test renderer feasibility, latent ceiling, context value, learned-component utility, and target specificity.

PDS and custom pseudobulk diagnostics are secondary; they cannot overrule the official aggregate score.

Four choices that set it apart

Context as a state mix

A new cell type needs only its control cells to be described. No perturbation data from it is required.

Biology-aware priors

Regulatory graphs, pathway sets and protein embeddings let it reason about genes it has never seen perturbed.

Anchored output

Knockdown depth for the target is set explicitly. Other effects are added onto real control cells.

One judge

The official evaluator's aggregate score selects every checkpoint and setting. No home-made metric overrules it.

De-risked at every step

G_RCan the renderer carry signal at all? Fed true effects, it must beat knockdown-only with a positive 95% lower bound. Checked before any model training.
G0Is the latent space expressive enough? An oracle in it must beat a PCA-50 baseline.
G1Does the learned model earn its complexity? It must beat knockdown-only, same-target transfer and shared-response baselines on held-out contexts, or the system falls back to the best simple baseline.
G2Is it target-specific? It must beat shuffled targets.

All gates run on leave-one-context-out evaluation, with at least four independent source contexts and nested selection so outer folds are never tuned on.
